About Nigeria

Nigeria is the most populous country in Africa and the seventh most populous in the world, with over 220 million people. Located in West Africa, it is home to more than 250 ethnic groups and a rich cultural heritage spanning thousands of years.

Abuja, the federal capital, is a planned city that became Nigeria's capital in 1991. Situated in the heart of the country, it is known for its modern architecture, wide boulevards, and the iconic Aso Rock.
