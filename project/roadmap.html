Practice Project Ideas

Building real projects is the fastest way to reinforce your skills and create a portfolio that stands out to employers. Here are four beginner-friendly project ideas you can start with free public data.

💰 Personal Finance Tracker

Download your bank statements as a CSV file, import them into Excel or Python, and analyze your spending by category over three months. Create a simple dashboard showing where your money goes.

⚽ Sports Statistics Analysis

Choose a sport you enjoy and find a dataset on Kaggle or FBref.com. Use SQL or Python to explore player and team performance trends, then visualize your findings in a chart or dashboard.

🌍 COVID-19 Data Dashboard

Use the publicly available Our World in Data COVID-19 dataset to visualize case trends, vaccination rates, or regional comparisons over time. This is a well-structured dataset ideal for beginners.

🛒 E-commerce Sales Report

Download a sample retail dataset from Kaggle and use SQL and a visualization tool to identify top-selling products, busiest sales periods, and regional sales differences.
